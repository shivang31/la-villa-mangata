About this place

A two-storey private villa on a farmhouse-style lawn near Mangata Jungle Safari, 25 km from Durg and an easy drive from Bhilai and Raipur. One group books the whole property: four bedrooms, a private pool that's lit at night, a covered gazebo and a lawn for events.

The space

Each bedroom has a king bed, an extra mattress, an attached bathroom with a Western toilet and its own balcony; two look over the garden and two over the pool. Indoors there's a lounge with sofas. Outside: a poolside deck with seating, a gazebo with a long dining table, and an open lawn.

Guest access

The whole villa, pool, gazebo, lawn and parking for 4 cars.

Food

Breakfast, lunch and dinner, veg and non-veg, are available from a fixed menu at extra cost. A floating breakfast can be served in the pool.

Questions guests ask

How much does La Villa Mangata cost per night or per person?

Rates depend on your dates, group size and any event, so we share a quote on WhatsApp. Send your dates to +91 91113 33368. You always get the whole villa for your group; meals, the floating breakfast and the projector are extra.

Is it a farmhouse with a private swimming pool near Bhilai and Durg?

Yes. It's a private villa on a large farmhouse-style lawn with its own swimming pool, about 25 km from Durg and close to Bhilai. One group books it at a time, so the pool, lawn and gazebo are never shared.

Can we host a pool party, birthday party, haldi or mehendi?

Yes. The lawn holds up to 100 guests standing or 80 seated, and you can bring your own decorator, caterer and DJ.

Is non-veg food allowed?

Yes. Both veg and non-veg food are allowed. Meals from the fixed menu cost extra, and for events you can bring your own caterer.

Can we book it for a one-day picnic or day outing?

Yes. Families, schools and companies book it for day outings. Message us on WhatsApp with the date and group size for day rates.

How far is it from Bhilai, Durg, Raipur and Rajnandgaon?

It's near Mangata Jungle Safari in Jhuradabri, Rajnandgaon district. Murhipar railway station is 4.5 km away and Durg and Rajnandgaon stations are about 25 km away, so it's an easy weekend getaway from Bhilai and Raipur.

Is La Villa Mangata couple friendly?

Yes. Unmarried couples and all-male groups are welcome. The primary guest must be 18 or older and every adult needs a photo ID; local IDs are accepted.

How do I book, and what's the contact number?

Pick your dates in the booking calendar and send the request on WhatsApp, or call +91 91113 33368. We confirm availability and the advance payment there.
